# Morocco Trek Tours — Pricing Review (Draft)

Updated: 24 October 2026

## How to read the prices

- All amounts are in EUR.
- The **1 traveler** tier is the total price for the solo booking.
- The **2–3**, **4–7**, and **8–12** tiers are prices per person.
- The matrix assumes a private/customized booking whose shared vehicle/guide costs are divided among the listed travelers. The current website does not provide a separate shared-versus-private selector, so confirm this assumption and the included services before publishing.
- Prices below are recommended market-positioning rates, not audited operating-cost calculations. Accommodation category, included meals, guide costs, transport, commissions, and supplier costs were not provided, so confirm those costs before publishing these rates as final business tariffs.
- The seasonal selector remains only on Mount Toubkal because the existing approved Toubkal price matrix distinguishes winter and summer. Other tour pages use one rate per itinerary and group tier.
- Mount Toubkal prices remain the previously approved values for the 2-day itinerary only. Other durations are quote-only.

## Important limitations

The market research supports useful benchmarks for Toubkal, Merzouga/Sahara, Ouzoud, and 10-day Morocco tours. It does not establish exact current supplier costs or identical competitor products for every specialist trek and custom itinerary. Prices for those routes are provisional recommendations derived from trip length and the relative service level; they should be reviewed against the actual itinerary inclusions and local supplier quotes before being treated as final.

No tests or browser checks were run, as requested.
